Hyaluronic acid is a naturally occurring molecule that can hold many times its weight in water, making it a star ingredient for hydration and plumping. But not all HA serums are created equal: molecular weight, concentration, and supporting ingredients all affect how well a formula penetrates and feels on the skin. Whether you have dry, oily, or sensitive skin, choosing the right serum depends on texture preferences, budget, and whether you want extra benefits like barrier repair or soothing. When shopping, look beyond the percentage hype. A well-formulated serum with multiple molecular weights can hydrate both the surface and deeper layers. Also consider your routine, HA works best applied to damp skin and sealed with a moisturizer.
