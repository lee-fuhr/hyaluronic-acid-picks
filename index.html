How we choose our picks

Our selections are based on a combination of brand reputation, long-term owner feedback from forums and verified-review aggregators, and published expert analyses from dermatologists and cosmetic chemists. We prioritize products that have maintained consistent quality over several years and have a low incidence of negative reports (irritation, pilling under sunscreen, or separate/crystallization issues). We ignore one-off influencer hype and instead look for patterns: a serum that users consistently repurchase and that professionals recommend across different skin types. What actually breaks in this category? Poor formulation can lead to sticky residue, balling up under other products, or over-hydration that paradoxically leaves skin tight. We watch for complaints about irritating preservatives, high alcohol content, or unstable packaging.
